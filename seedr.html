Seeder: the best torrent download helper!

I recently discovered Seeder, a tool that lets you quickly download a torrent to their servers, so you can download from them as if it was a direct download!

    Seeder is great for:
  • People (like me!) who can't port forward, and therefore find a lot of files with 0 seeders
  • People who want to stream torrents! You can copy a download link and use it to stream from your favourite player!

Feel free to give it a try! Link's https://www.seedr.cc/
